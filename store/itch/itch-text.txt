=== Short description / tagline (itch.io field, max. ~100 characters) ===

Bounce from wall to wall, dodge the spikes and collect coins!


=== Page text (short, English) ===

Tap or press a key to jump – your chicken bounces from wall to wall, and every wall is full of spikes. It gets faster with every bounce. How far can you get?

Collect coins to unlock new chickens, backgrounds and chiptune tracks.

Controls: X / Space / click to jump · arrow keys + X in menus · M toggles music
Play it in your browser or download it for Windows, Linux or Android (ad-free).

Sammle Münzen und schalte neue Hühner, Hintergründe und Chiptune-Musik frei.

Steuerung: X / Leertaste / Klick zum Springen · Pfeiltasten + X im Menü · M schaltet die Musik um
